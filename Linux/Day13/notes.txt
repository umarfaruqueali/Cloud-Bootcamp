========== Day 13 : Cron Jobs ==========

CRON
- Cron is a Linux service used to execute commands or scripts automatically at scheduled times.

CRON JOB
- A Cron Job is a scheduled task executed automatically by the Cron service.

CRONTAB
- Crontab (Cron Table) stores all scheduled Cron Jobs of a user.

CRON SYNTAX

Minute Hour Day-of-Month Month Day-of-Week Command

Example:
0 2 * * * /home/umar/Cloud-Bootcamp/Linux/Day13/cron_test.sh

Meaning:
0  → Minute
2  → Hour
*  → Every Day of Month
*  → Every Month
*  → Every Day of Week

* means "Every"

OUTPUT REDIRECTION

>   → Overwrites the file.

>>  → Appends output to the end of the file.

Cron executes jobs in the background.
Output is usually redirected to a log file.
