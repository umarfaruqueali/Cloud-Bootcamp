# DAY 51 — EBS Snapshots

EBS Snapshot:
- Point-in-time backup/recovery point of an EBS volume.
- Used to protect data before risky changes or for recovery.

Why Snapshots?
- Protect important EBS data.
- Recover data after accidental deletion or corruption.
- Create new EBS volumes from a snapshot.

Volume vs Snapshot:
- EBS Volume → Working storage attached to EC2.
- EBS Snapshot → Point-in-time recovery copy of a volume.

Snapshot behavior:
- First snapshot stores the required data.
- Later snapshots store changed blocks incrementally.

Recovery Flow:
Snapshot
   ↓
Create new EBS Volume
   ↓
Attach Volume to EC2
   ↓
Mount Volume
   ↓
Access/Recover Data

Important:
- A snapshot cannot be directly attached to an EC2 instance.
- A new EBS volume must first be created from the snapshot.
