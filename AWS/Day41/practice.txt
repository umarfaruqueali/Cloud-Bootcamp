DAY 41 - IAM POLICIES & ROLES PRACTICE

OBJECTIVE
Create read-only and admin policies and understand IAM roles,
trust policies and permission policies.

1. CUSTOMER-MANAGED POLICIES

Created:

CloudBootcamp-S3-ReadOnly
→ Customer managed policy.

CloudBootcamp-S3-Admin
→ Customer managed policy.

These policies were created to practice defining permissions
for AWS resources.

2. IAM GROUP

Created:

ReadOnlyUsers

The group provides a structure for assigning permissions
centrally to read-only users.

3. IAM ROLE

Created:

CloudBootcamp-EC2-S3ReadOnly

Trusted entity:
EC2

The role uses a trust policy allowing EC2 to assume the role.

Attached permissions policy:

CloudBootcamp-S3-ReadOnly

4. TRUST POLICY

Observed the role trust policy:

Principal:
ec2.amazonaws.com

Action:
sts:AssumeRole

Meaning:

EC2
→ Trusted entity
→ Can assume the role.

5. PERMISSIONS POLICY

The role has:

CloudBootcamp-S3-ReadOnly

This policy determines what the role can do after it
has been assumed.

6. POLICY SIMULATOR

Test 1:

S3 → ListAllMyBuckets

Result:
Allowed

Reason:
Explicit allow in one policy statement.

Test 2:

S3 → DeleteBucket

Result:
Denied

Reason:
Implicit deny due to no matching statement.

7. KEY OBSERVATION

A role's trust policy and permissions policy have different jobs.

Trust Policy
→ Determines who can assume the role.

Permissions Policy
→ Determines what the role can do.

8. SAFETY

The role was tested using IAM Policy Simulator.

No EC2 instance was launched and no billable compute
resource was created.
